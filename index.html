ABOUT B2B HACKS · OCTOBER 31, 2026

A focused day to build practical B2B solutions.

B2B Hacks is a 6-hour, state-level hackathon at Nanospace, Nanakramguda in Hyderabad. Bring an idea, meet co-builders, and make a useful prototype for a real business problem.

01

Choose a real problem

Start with a business workflow or customer need worth improving.

02

Build a prototype

Pick one track and shape an idea into something people can understand or try.

03

Show the impact

Explain who it helps, how it works, and what becomes easier or better.

THREE WAYS TO BUILD

OCTOBER 31 · 8:00 AM THROUGH CLOSING · 6 BUILD HOURS

A full day. Six focused hours to build.

Check-in begins at 8:00 AM. The build is split into two sessions, with mentoring in the afternoon, followed by judging and awards.

  1. 8:00–9:30 AM
    REGISTRATION

    Participant check-in

    Register, get settled, and prepare for the day.

  2. 9:30–10:30 AM
    WELCOME

    Introduction, rules & guidelines

    Opening remarks and a briefing on the hackathon format, rules, and guidelines.

  3. 10:30 AM–1:00 PM
    BUILD · 2.5 HOURS

    Hackathon session one

    Start building your team's solution and shape the first working prototype.

  4. 1:00–2:30 PM
    BREAK

    Lunch

    Lunch break before the afternoon build session.

  5. 2:30–6:00 PM
    BUILD + MENTORING · 3.5 HOURS

    Hackathon session two

    Continue building with mentoring support and refine your prototype.

  6. 6:00–6:40 PM
    REFRESHMENTS

    Refreshment break

    A short break before teams prepare for judging.

  7. 6:40–7:30 PM
    TRANSITION

    Demo preparation & judging setup

    50-minute buffer before the main judging session; exact activities are to be confirmed.

  8. Around 7:30–9:30 PM
    MAIN JUDGING

    Project presentations & judging

    Teams present their work and judges review the projects.

  9. After 9:30 PM
    CLOSING

    Awards & closing

    Awards, closing remarks, and wrap-up. End time to be announced.

Build time totals 6 hours: 2.5 hours in session one and 3.5 hours in session two. Check-in through the end of judging spans about 13.5 hours; the closing follows. Main judging time is approximate.

A few things to know

What is B2B Hacks?

A 6-hour, state-level hackathon at Nanospace, Nanakramguda, where builders develop practical B2B solutions and show what their prototypes can do. The registration fee is ₹400.

When and where is it happening?

B2B Hacks is on October 31, 2026, at Nanospace, Nanakramguda, Hyderabad.

What are the tracks?

GenAI, Agentic AI, and Open Innovation. See what each track covers.

What are the prizes?

The total prize pool is ₹30,000. Prize distribution details will be announced by the organizers.

Is the exact schedule available?

The build totals six hours across two sessions. Registration begins at 8:00 AM; main judging is planned for around 7:30–9:30 PM, with closing afterward.

Who can participate, and how do I register?

Teams of 1–4 can apply. Choose one of the three tracks in the application form, then submit your team details.

Who is organizing B2B Hacks?

The event is hosted by BacktoBase and co-hosted by Code Viveks (SVIT).

Official challenge statements, eligibility, and detailed timings will be updated here when confirmed.
